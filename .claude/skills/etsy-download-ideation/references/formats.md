# Format catalog

Price anchors below are **heuristics to verify against live Etsy comparables**, not
market data. Label them as estimates every time you use one. Verify before quoting
them to the user as fact.

| Format | Typical build | Anchor (verify) | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Single printable (1–2 pp) | 1–2 h | $3–6 | Treadmill. Only as a bundle component or loss-leader. |
| Fillable PDF form | 3–5 h | $8–15 | Saves the buyer printing. Real perceived value lift over flat PDF. |
| Multi-page workbook (15–40 pp) | 1–3 days | $12–25 | The workhorse tier. Needs a genuine through-line, not padding. |
| Bundle / system | assembly | $20–45 | Highest margin per unit of new work. Build from proven singles. |
| Notion / Google Sheets template | 4–8 h | $10–25 | Delivered as a duplicate link. Check Etsy's current digital-delivery rules. |
| Editable Canva template | 3–6 h | $10–20 | Verify every embedded asset's commercial license first. |
| Professional-tier pack (clinician/educator) | 1–3 days | $25–60 | Best price ceiling; smallest audience; strongest moat. State the audience in the listing. |
| Card deck / cut-out set (print at home) | 1–2 days | $12–22 | Sensory and communication use cases. Ink-light version is mandatory. |
| Poster / visual reference | 2–4 h | $5–12 | Commodity unless the content itself is non-obvious. |
| Audio or guided script (PDF + MP3) | 1–2 days | $15–30 | Check Etsy's rules for non-PDF digital delivery before committing. |

## Audience × moment grid

Cross these when generating. The productive cells are the ones where the moment is
acute and the existing market is generic.

**Audiences:** late-diagnosed autistic adult · AuDHD adult · parent of a PDA kid ·
autistic teen · partner or family member · teacher / SENCO · fellow clinician

**Moments:** burnout onset and recovery · post-diagnosis disorientation ·
school refusal and Monday mornings · demand-avoidance spiral · requesting workplace
or school accommodations · sensory overload · executive stall and task initiation ·
appointment and IEP prep · explaining yourself to family

## Seasonality

- **Jan** — new year, systems, "start over" energy. Frame carefully: this niche is
  hostile to resolution culture, and that hostility is itself a positioning angle.
- **Jul–Aug** — back to school. The strongest window for parent- and teacher-facing items.
- **Sep–Oct** — school refusal, early-term collapse, first IEP cycles.
- **Apr** — Autism Acceptance Month. High traffic, high scrutiny. Nothing that reads
  as awareness-ribbon content.
- **Nov–Dec** — holiday sensory and family-navigation content; demand is real and
  under-served.

List seasonal items 6–8 weeks ahead so Etsy's ranking has time to settle.
