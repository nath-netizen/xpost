# Validation — how to score each criterion

Score 1–5. Name the source for each. If a criterion was guessed, write `GUESS`
next to it in the card. A card with more than two `GUESS` marks is not ready.

## Demand signal (×3)

Source: eRank, EverBee, Alura, or Marmalead keyword export. Etsy's Open API does
**not** provide search volume, competitor sales, or analytics — Open API v3 never
carried over the v2 stats endpoints — so this number cannot come from the API.
Verify current field names against the tool's UI; they change.

What you want per keyword: average monthly searches, average clicks, competition
(listing count), and click-through rate.

- **5** — meaningful search volume, listing count low enough that a good new listing
  can surface, and the top results are visibly weak.
- **3** — real volume, crowded, but the top results are generic.
- **1** — no measurable volume, or a keyword with six-figure listing counts.

Also check Etsy search directly: type the keyword, read the first two rows. If the
top sellers have thousands of sales on a near-identical item, the search-volume
number is a trap.

## Differentiation (×3)

The test: could a competent generalist with Canva and no clinical background produce
a materially equivalent version in under an hour? If yes, score 1, regardless of how
good the idea feels.

- **5** — the product encodes judgment, sequencing, or framing that a non-specialist
  would get wrong in a way a buyer in this niche would notice.
- **1** — a layout, a font pairing, and a list anyone could write.

## Credibility leverage (×2)

Does the clinical background do work here, or is it decoration? Distinguish
"I can make this faster" (no leverage) from "I know why the standard version of this
harms PDA kids" (leverage).

## Production economy (×2)

Hours to a sellable v1, not to perfection.
**5** = under ~3 hours. **3** = a weekend. **1** = original illustration at volume.
Be honest about revision rounds and accessibility passes; they are not free.

## Price ceiling (×2)

What can this defensibly list at, given what the shop's own CSVs show has converted?

Fee reality per sale: $0.20 listing, 6.5% transaction, payment processing
(roughly 3% + $0.25 in the US), plus Offsite Ads where applicable — 15% for shops
under $10k in trailing-year sales with opt-out available, 12% and mandatory above
$10k. Verify against Etsy's current fee page; these move.

A $4 item nets roughly $3.20 before ads. Under $8 is a volume treadmill; say so.

- **5** — defensibly $15+, usually a bundle, a system, or a professional-audience item.
- **1** — capped at $3–5 by what comparable listings charge.

## Ladder potential (×1)

Does it open a series, a bundle, a seasonal refresh, or a professional-tier upsell?
Terminal one-offs score 1.

## Before building anything

Run these, in order:
1. The keyword in the research tool — volume and competition.
2. The keyword in Etsy search — read the top two rows and their sales counts.
3. The shop's own CSVs — has anything adjacent already sold, or already failed?

If step 2 contradicts step 1, trust step 2.
