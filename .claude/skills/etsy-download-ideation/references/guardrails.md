# Guardrails

Four binary gates. Any failure kills the idea. Report as `KILLED — G<n>` with one
sentence of reason. Do not soften, do not propose a workaround unless the workaround
is itself a materially different product.

## G1 — Scope of practice

The shop is authored by a clinician. That is the asset and also the liability: a
product that reads as individualized clinical care creates duty-of-care exposure and
may cross state licensure lines, because a download reaches buyers anywhere.

**Kill:**
- Anything that screens, scores, or suggests a diagnosis — including "am I autistic?"
  checklists, PDA self-tests, burnout severity scales with cutoffs.
- Medication content that implies individual direction: dosing tables, titration
  schedules, cross-taper guides, "what to ask for" framed as a recommendation.
- Crisis, self-harm, or suicidality workbooks sold unsupervised.
- Anything promising an outcome ("reverse burnout in 30 days").

**Allow:**
- Education about a concept, clearly general ("what demand avoidance is and isn't").
- Organizational and communication tools the buyer fills in themselves
  (accommodation request builders, appointment prep sheets, sensory profile
  *self-description* logs with no scoring).
- Explicitly professional-facing resources sold to clinicians and educators, where
  the audience is stated in the listing.

Every product carries a short scope line: educational, not medical advice, not a
substitute for individualized care. Put it in the PDF, not only the listing.

## G2 — Intellectual property

**Never reproduce, adapt, or "inspire-by" a proprietary instrument.** This is the most
common way a clinician-run shop gets a takedown or worse. Assume every named
standardized measure is licensed unless proven otherwise, and proving otherwise is
the user's job, not yours to assume.

Treat as off-limits by default: Conners, BASC, SRS, ADOS, ADI-R, Vineland, CARS,
M-CHAT derivatives, EDA-Q, RAADS-R, CAT-Q, AQ, ASRS, SNAP-IV, Vanderbilt, and any
other named scale. Some are free to *use* under stated terms; free to use is not the
same as free to reformat and resell, and the resale is exactly what an Etsy listing is.

Also kill:
- Reselling or lightly editing third-party templates — explicitly banned by Etsy's
  Creativity Standards for digital downloads since June 2025.
- Figures, tables, or diagrams lifted from papers, books, or other sites.
- Fonts, clipart, or stock assets whose license does not permit commercial
  redistribution in an end product. Check the license, not the vibe.
- Character, brand, school-district, or institutional IP.

## G3 — Community integrity

Carry over the voice rules in `voice.md`. A product that sells well and treats the
community badly is still a kill.

- Identity-first language ("autistic person", "PDAer") unless a specific audience
  clearly prefers otherwise.
- No functioning labels. No "severity" framing for autism itself.
- No cure, recovery, treatment-for-autism, or normalization framing.
- No inspiration porn, no pity, no parent-suffering-centered content that positions
  the autistic person as the burden.
- No compliance-based behavior tools. In a PDA-adjacent shop, reward charts,
  token economies, and "following instructions" trackers are actively off-brand
  and will be called out publicly by the audience.
- Never speak for the whole community. Speak to an experience.

## G4 — Originality and AI disclosure

Etsy's Creativity Standards were extended explicitly to digital downloads on
2025-06-10, and enforcement tightened materially through 2025–2026.

- The user must be the designer. AI-assisted work is permitted but places the item
  under "Designed by" rather than "Made by", and requires disclosure.
- Disclose AI involvement where Etsy asks for it and in the description. Under-
  disclosure is the enforcement trigger, not AI use itself.
- If an idea only works by mass-generating near-identical variants, kill it. That is
  the pattern Etsy is removing listings for.

Verify current policy wording at the time of listing — policy in this area has moved
repeatedly and will again.
